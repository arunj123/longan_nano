2. SIPEED LONGAN NANO (GD32VF103CBT6) - HARDWARE & GEOMETRY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 Mechanical Dimensions & PCB Geometry:
- Overall PCB Length: 46.10 mm
- Overall PCB Width: 20.00 mm
- PCB Substrate Thickness: 1.00 mm (FR4, red soldermask)
- Sub-segment Lengths:
  * Left edge to center notch: 19.60 mm
  * Center notch width: 3.80 mm (semi-circular side index cutouts)
  * Center notch to right edge: 22.60 mm (19.60 + 3.80 + 22.60 = 46.00 ~ 46.10 mm)
- Pin Header Footprint:
  * Dual-row DIP layout, standard 2.54 mm (100 mil) pitch
  * Row-to-row spacing: 17.78 mm (700 mil / 0.70 in), standard breadboard span
  * Pin count: 34 pins total (2 rows x 17 pins)
  * Left pin group: 8 pins per row
  * Center notch: occupies gap equivalent to ~1 pin pitch
  * Right pin group: 9 pins per row

2.2 Vertical Component Stackup (Z-Axis):
- Top Side Components (above PCB top surface):
  * USB Type-C Receptacle: 3.16 mm H x 8.94 mm W, extends ~2.80-3.00 mm past PCB edge
  * 0.96" IPS RGB LCD Module: ~2.80-3.00 mm total height (includes foam tape)
  * Tactile SMD Buttons (BOOT0 & RESET): ~2.20-2.50 mm body height; 0.30 mm travel
- Bottom Side Components (below PCB bottom surface):
  * MicroSD (TF) Card Socket: 1.80 mm thick x 11.50 mm wide x 12.00 mm long.
    Push-pull slot opening faces left edge (opposite USB-C).
  * Soldered Pin Headers: Standard straight headers extend ~6.00 mm below PCB
    (+2.50 mm black plastic spacer). If headers are omitted/flush-soldered,
    clearance requirement below PCB is ~2.20 mm.
- Enclosure Internal Height Clearance:
  * With through-hole headers: ~12.50 mm minimum internal Z height.
  * Without headers (flush / low-profile): ~6.50 mm minimum internal Z height.

2.3 0.96" IPS LCD Display Specifications & Case Cutout:
- Controller & Bus: ST7735 SPI LCD (SPI0 peripheral, 8-pin 0.5 mm pitch FPC)
- Resolution: 160 x 80 RGB pixels
- Active Display Window Area: 21.70 mm L x 10.80 mm W (0.96" diagonal)
- Outer LCD Body & Glass Dimensions (Verified on 2.54 mm PCB pin grid & case_rev3_assembled_top.jpg):
  * Total outer LCD module body: 28.00 mm length x 13.50 mm breadth (X_pcb = 7.40..35.40 mm)
  * Left black plastic bezel step: X_pcb = 7.40 mm to 10.50 mm (3.10 mm step)
  * Smooth black LCD glass top surface: X_pcb = 10.50 mm to 35.40 mm (24.90 mm length),
    width 13.50 mm, centered at Y_pcb = 10.00 mm.
- Recommended Case Window Opening: 27.20 mm L x 11.40 mm W inner (28.00 x 12.60 mm beveled, centered at X_pcb = 21.10 mm); underside pocket 28.90 x 15.20 x 1.40 mm.
- CRITICAL FPC Flex Cable Relief:
  * Ribbon folds over the left edge of display glass (X_pcb = 3.00..8.00 mm).
  * DO NOT clamp or compress the folded flex cable.
  * Upper lid MUST incorporate an internal relief pocket of at least 5.0 mm x 8.5 mm
    with 1.40 mm depth to avoid damaging delicate flex traces.

2.4 Port & Button Locations:
- USB Type-C: Centered on right edge (20.0 mm width). Wall cutout: 11.5 mm W x 4.8 mm H.
- BOOT0 Button: Between Pin 14 (B13) & Pin 15 (B12) at X_pcb = 37.50 mm, Y_pcb = 13.30 mm. Cutout: D = 4.0 mm.
- RESET Button: Between Pin 14 (B1) & Pin 15 (B10) at X_pcb = 37.50 mm, Y_pcb = 6.45 mm. Cutout: D = 4.0 mm.
- MicroSD Slot: Bottom side (omitted on enclosure wall when USB MSC Drive Mode is used).
- JTAG Pads: 4 pads on left edge (3V3 R0 T0 GND), 2.54 mm pitch; leave >= 12.0 mm gap in left cradle for 4-pin header.

3. CURRENT SENSOR BREAKOUT MODULES (INA219)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 VARIANT A: BLUE INA219 BREAKOUT (Standard with Screw Terminal Block)
- Function: High-side DC voltage (0-26V) and bidirectional current (3.2A) monitor over I2C.
- Mechanical Dimensions (Verified in case_rev3_internal_layout.jpg & ina219_blue_top_view.jpg):
  * PCB Width (along 6-pin header & VIN-/VIN+ edge, X-axis): 25.50 mm (1.00")
  * PCB Length (from VCC..VIN+ edge to VIN-/VIN+ edge, Y-axis): 22.30 mm (0.88")
  * PCB Thickness: 1.60 mm
  * Total Height with Screw Terminal: ~12.00 mm
  * Total Height without Terminal (flat SMD + wire loops): ~3.20-7.50 mm
- Mounting Holes:
  * 4 corner holes: D = 2.60-2.80 mm
  * Center spacing: 20.40 mm (X width, 8 x 2.54 mm) x 17.20 mm (Y length)
  * Recommended Standoffs: 4.0 mm height (clears bottom solder joints) with tapered D = 1.9->1.5 mm poles
- Connectors:
  * Screw Terminal / Sense Pads: 2-pin 3.5 mm pitch (VIN+ supply input, VIN- load output).
    Cutout: 11.5 mm W x 7.5 mm H wire window + 2x 4.0 mm access holes on lid.
  * Logic Header: 1x6 pin 2.54 mm pitch:
    1: VCC (+3.0V to +5.5V, connects to Longan Nano 3V3)
    2: GND (Ground, connects to Longan Nano GND)
    3: SCL (I2C Clock, connects to Longan Nano PB6)
    4: SDA (I2C Data, connects to Longan Nano PB7)
    5: VIN- (Negative sense / Load path)
    6: VIN+ (Positive sense / Supply path)
- Electrical Characteristics:
  * IC: Texas Instruments INA219B (SOIC-8 or SOT-23-8)
  * Shunt Resistor: R100 = 0.100 Ohm, 1%, 2W, 2512 SMD package (6.3 x 3.2 mm)
  * Target Bus Voltage: 0 to +26 VDC (High-side sensing)
  * Current Measurement Range:
    - Full-scale: +/-3.20 A with 0.80 mA resolution (12-bit ADC @ +/-320 mV PGA)
    - High-precision: +/-400 mA with 0.10 mA resolution (div8 gain)
  * Accuracy: +/-1%
  * Operating Current: ~1.0 mA (operating), 15 uA (sleep)
  * I2C Addresses (via A0/A1 solder jumpers): 0x40 (default), 0x41, 0x44, 0x45

3.2 VARIANT B: RED INA219 BREAKOUT (Ultra-Compact Miniaturized Variant)
- Function: Ultra-compact high-side DC monitor without screw terminal.
- Mechanical Dimensions:
  * PCB Length: 20.00 mm
  * PCB Width: 16.40 mm
  * PCB Thickness: 1.60 mm
  * Total Height: ~3.00 mm (flat SMD, low-profile)
  * Mounting Holes: 2 corner holes (D = 2.0 mm)
- Electrical Characteristics:
  * IC: INA219 (SOT-23-8)
  * Supply Voltage: 3.3V - 5.5V DC
  * Operating Current: ~1.0 mA (operating), 15 uA (sleep)
  * Current Range: +/-3.20 A (12-bit resolution, 0.1 Ohm shunt, +/-1% accuracy)
  * Operating Temperature: -25 deg C to +85 deg C
  * Connectors: Direct solder pads / pin header pads (no screw terminal)
  * Application: Ideal for ultra-compact inline dongle cases or cable-mounted housings.

  * Longan Nano on top lid (LCD facing up), INA219 mounted below.
  * 8.0 mm vertical standoff clearance between boards.
- Topology 3 (Inline Cable Dongle with Red INA219):
  * Dimensions: ~52.0 mm L x ~24.0 mm W x ~12.0 mm H
  * Ultra-compact inline profile with wire strain reliefs.

4.2 Critical Mechanical Tolerances for 3D Printing (FDM / SLA):
- Wall Thickness: 1.80 - 2.00 mm (minimum 4 perimeters with 0.4 mm nozzle).
- PCB Pocket Clearance: 0.50 - 0.60 mm per side (1.0 - 1.2 mm total).
- 360-Deg Lid Skirt Clearance: 0.25 mm per side (locks lid against X/Y sliding).
- LCD Window Opening: 27.20 mm L x 11.40 mm W inner (28.00 x 12.60 mm outer bevel, centered at X_pcb = 21.10 mm).
- LCD Module Pocket: 28.90 mm L x 15.20 mm W x 1.40 mm deep recess in lid (fits 13.5 x 28.0 mm LCD).
- FPC Flex Relief Notch: 5.0 mm x 8.5 mm x 1.40 mm deep void over display ribbon.
- USB-C Cutout: 11.5 mm W x 4.8 mm H on right end-wall.
- Sense Wire Cutout: 11.5 mm W x 8.2 mm H wire window + 2x 4.0 mm access holes.
- Button Cutouts: 2x 4.0 mm chamfered holes at X_pcb = 37.50 mm, Y_pcb = 6.45 & 13.30 mm.
- INA219 Standoffs: 20.40 mm (X) x 17.20 mm (Y) spacing, 4.0 mm height, tapered D = 1.9->1.5 mm poles.
- Thermal Dissipation: 4x ventilation slots (1.6 x 8.0 mm) above R100 shunt (1W at 3.2A).
